What is on the screen

  
Every object below is read from the deposited 9GC8 chain A coordinate frame. The generator was
  conditioned on a 20-residue cut of that pocket and its output was mapped straight back by adding
  the pocket centroid, so ligands and receptor share an origin with no alignment applied here.

Two things the brief assumed that the files do not support

  

    

      

        
There are no docked poses for D1 or E

        validation/results/poses/ holds four files — armA, armB, armC and armD2.
        armD1_docked.sdf and armE_docked.sdf were never written, and
        logs/dock_armE.log stops after its header line. The docked pose source is therefore
        disabled for those two arms here.
      

      

        
D1 does not need them

        generate_arms.py maps every conditioned arm back into the crystal frame by adding its own
        conditioning-pocket centroid, decoy included. D1's molecules are already in the 9GC8 frame — they
        sit on the decoy site, 18 Å from the true pocket, which is what that control is for. Load it
        against the full chain to see both sites at once.
      

    

    

      

        
Arm E has no pocket frame to return to

        Arm E is the unconditional negative control, so the mapping step is skipped by design and its 957
        molecules stay centred on the model origin, 37 Å away. Docking was the intended fix and it
        did not produce a file, so no honest pocket-frame pose exists for arm E today. The viewer offers a
        rigid shift of the whole ensemble onto the pocket centroid for size and shape comparison only — it is
        a viewing aid, never a pose, and it is labelled as such on the stage.
      

      

        
What is shown per molecule

        Heavy atoms only. Per-molecule metrics exist for arms A, B, C and D2 — the four scored conditions in
        results/joined.csv — and are index-aligned with both the generated and the docked SDF.
        D1, E and the ChEMBL control carry geometry but no scores.
